# 02 — Business Model (for Agnela)

> Agnela owns the business model — this doc is a proposal to react to, not a decision.

## Options considered
- (a) Freemium → subscription (owner pays)
- (b) Per-pet add-on / overage
- (c) B2B2C SaaS for clinics / groomers / boarders (they invite + subsidize)
- (d) Transactions: travel pack, lost-pet QR tag, claim packet

## Recommended tiers
- **Free:** 1 pet, 25 docs, 1 active share link. Goal: activation + sharing loop.
- **Plus $6.99/mo or $69/yr:** up to 5 pets, unlimited docs, reminders, custom QR/passport.
  Anchors against insurance ($30–60/mo) — $69/yr feels trivial.
- **Family $9.99/mo:** 10 pets, co-owners (post-MVP).
- **Overage:** $2/mo per extra pet beyond plan (no penalty for single-pet owners).
- **B2B2C — Clinic Starter $49/mo, Pro $149/mo/location:** branded intake, cert push into
  owner vaults, due-list recall (churn killer for clinics). This is the distribution wedge.
- **Transactions (later):** travel cert pack $9, QR collar tag $19, claim packet $5.

## Why this mix
Owners pay for peace of mind (never lapsed, never scrambling); clinics pay for
retained visits + less front-desk typing; insurers/airlines are channel partners later.
Free is deliberately share-shaped: every passport link is an ad.
